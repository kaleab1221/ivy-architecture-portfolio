// PRACTICE PROFILE

Pragmatic Architecture & Precision Engineering for a Rapidly Urbanizing World

IVY Construction & Trading Co. Ltd. operates at the confluence of visionary architectural design, rigorous structural engineering, and heavy-duty general construction.

With landmark projects spanning Ethiopia, South Sudan, and Iraq, our multidisciplinary practice resolves complex programmatic, climatic, and logistical challenges. From 45-story commercial arch skyscrapers and university medical campuses to remote modular energy camps and agricultural processing hubs, we turn ambitious concepts into enduring physical realities.

Our unique integration of architectural design with direct contracting and international material trading ensures zero friction between conceptual geometry and precision on-site execution.
