Practice Introduction

A multidisciplinary practice bridging visionary architectural conception with structural precision and turnkey site execution.

Founded on the belief that enduring architecture requires seamless integration between design and construction, IVY brings together architects, civil engineers, project managers, and international supply specialists under a unified practice.

By eliminating friction between abstract blueprints and ground-level logistics, we ensure that complex geometry, high-performance structural systems, and refined interior details are realized on schedule, within budget, and to international quality standards.

From 45-story civic arch towers in Addis Ababa to high-security defense headquarters and remote modular industrial installations in South Sudan, our work responds directly to the climate, geography, and urban realities of the territories we shape.

Integrated Capabilities

Our in-house capabilities span the full lifecycle of development—from site feasibility and masterplanning through structural analysis, turnkey contracting, and interior fit-out.

Civil & Structural Engineering

Company Profile

Architecture Backed by Engineering Discipline

IVY Construction & Trading Co. Ltd. was established to address a critical market need across East Africa and the Middle East: the gap between ambitious architectural aspirations and the engineering discipline required to execute them reliably.

Headquartered in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with active regional operations in Juba, South Sudan, and cross-border logistics in Erbil, Iraq, IVY has successfully delivered over thirty-four landmark commissions across diverse climatic, topographical, and regulatory environments.

Our leadership comprises seasoned architects, structural engineers, cost surveyors, and certified construction superintendents whose collective portfolio encompasses commercial high-rises, national defense headquarters, regional hospitals, and university masterplans.
