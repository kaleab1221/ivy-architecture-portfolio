Hagbes Showroom & Machinery Sales Center

Aerodynamic Aerofoil Glass Pavilion

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Gelan, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

An architectural tour de force along the Addis-Adama industrial highway in Gelan. Designed for heavy machinery, agriculture equipment, and automotive sales, the pavilion features a monumental sweeping curved roof reminiscent of an airplane wing, enclosing a 16-meter column-free glass showroom concourse.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Sweeping aerodynamic curved steel roofline
  • Column-free structural glass curtain wall
  • Heavy equipment exhibition concourse
  • Outdoor tractor and excavator display boulevard
