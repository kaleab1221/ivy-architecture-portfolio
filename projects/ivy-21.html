PHCC General Hospital Complex

Emergency Medical Center & Clinical Inpatient Facility

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Juba, South Sudan

Architectural Brief & Concept

A vital regional hospital in Juba designed to international healthcare standards. Features a grand arched drop-off portico for ambulances, modern reception and triage zones, sterile pathology testing laboratories, and bright 24-bed acute inpatient wards with direct garden courtyard views.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Emergency ambulance drop-off portico
  • Certified sterile clinical testing labs
  • Inpatient recovery wards with green views
  • Independent backup solar and medical oxygen plants
