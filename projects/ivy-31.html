Villa Neoclassique Bishoftu

4-Story Classical Residence & Rooftop Pergola

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bishoftu, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A stately residence reflecting neoclassical symmetry fused with modern luxury. Features grand double-height Corinthian entrance columns, arched French balconies with wrought-iron railings, stone balustrades, and a rooftop pergola lounge.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Double-height neoclassical portico columns
  • Arched French balconies with decorative balustrades
  • Rooftop timber pergola viewing deck
  • Private ground floor carriage garage
