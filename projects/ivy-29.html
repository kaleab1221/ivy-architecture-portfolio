Bethelem Academy Comprehensive Campus

K-12 Educational Compound & Olympic Athletics Track

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bishoftu, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A progressive educational campus designed around a bold V-shaped academic building featuring vibrant polychromatic solar louvers. The grounds incorporate an Olympic running track, soccer pitch, tennis and basketball courts, early learning daycare facilities, and landscaped study courtyards.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Iconic V-shaped academic wing with rainbow louvers
  • Full regulation running track & soccer stadium
  • Dedicated kindergarten through preparatory zones
  • Internal tree-canopied play and assembly courtyards
