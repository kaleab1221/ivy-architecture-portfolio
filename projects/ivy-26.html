Kingdon Salt Processing Factory

Industrial Salt Refinery & Logistics Terminal

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Afedera, Afar, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

Positioned in the mineral-rich Afar salt flats near Lake Afedera. The facility is engineered for harsh desert conditions, comprising heavy truck intake scales, a 200-meter automated refining and iodization warehouse, administrative offices, and high-volume freight logistics dispatch.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • 200-meter clear-span automated refining warehouse
  • Heavy freight truck scale and staging loop
  • Desert-hardened corrosion resistant materials
  • Autonomous power generation and water desalinization
