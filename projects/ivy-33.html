Noora Cliffside Lake Resort

Volcanic Basalt Bluff Hospitality & Finishing Design

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bishoftu, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A daring feat of cliffside engineering perched atop the sheer volcanic basalt crags overlooking Bishoftu's crater lake. The design features stepped timber suites, thatched pavilion roofs celebrating Ethiopian vernacular craft, and cantilevered glass observation walkways suspended over the abyss.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Cantilevered cliff-hanging suite terraces
  • Traditional thatched conical roof pavilions
  • Panoramic crater lake infinity vistas
  • Engineered basalt rock anchoring systems
