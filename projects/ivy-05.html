Sevita Real Estate Tower

22-Story Commercial & Residential Skyscraper

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bole Road, Alem Building, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

Standing on prime Bole Road, the Sevita Tower is an iconic glass-and-steel skyscraper engineered to define the Addis Ababa skyline. The tower incorporates a bustling street-level restaurant terrace, luxury retail shopping podium, Grade-A corporate offices, and serviced residential apartments crowned by an illuminated sky crown.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Full-height monolithic curtain wall facade
  • Street-level cafe & pedestrian promenade
  • Integrated solar louvers and LED facade illumination
  • Multi-level subterranean automated parking
