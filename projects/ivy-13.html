Wachamo University Research Campus

Colonnaded Agriculture Lab & Postgraduate Center

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Hosanna, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A major expansion of Wachamo University in Hosanna, comprising a classical colonnaded Agricultural Laboratory with a central biophilic lightwell, a 6-story Postgraduate Academic Facility with outdoor access stair towers, and a specialized Poultry Research Laboratory.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Modern classical colonnade perimeter
  • Central lightwell atrium for natural ventilation
  • Specialized wet and dry research laboratories
  • External egress stairs with graphic architectural articulation
