GPOC Petrol VIP Residences & Modular Camp

Oil Field Accommodation & Pre-Engineered Compound

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Wizeen Camp / Unity State, South Sudan

Architectural Brief & Concept

Constructed in challenging remote conditions in South Sudan, this complex provides high-comfort accommodation for petroleum executives and technical staff. Consists of 2-story serviced VIP apartment blocks, stilt-mounted insulated prefab living quarters, central dining, sports, and healthcare facilities.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Climate-resilient elevated stilt modular units
  • VIP executive serviced apartment blocks
  • Autonomous power & water treatment network
  • Central community recreation and dining pavilions
