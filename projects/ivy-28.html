King Nejashi Islamic Center & Monumental Arch Tower

Monumental Grand Mosque & 45-Story Arch Skyscraper

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bulgaria / AU Area, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

An epochal cultural and architectural landmark for the African continent, situated near the African Union Headquarters in Addis Ababa. The project unites an awe-inspiring 45-story Arch Skyscraper featuring a soaring central Islamic arch void lined with intricate golden geometric mashrabiya filigree, alongside a grand congregational mosque with four towering 75-meter minarets, cultural conference center, and sweeping civic prayer plazas.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Iconic 45-story Arch Skyscraper with monumental arch void
  • Intricate gold mashrabiya geometric latticework
  • Grand congregational mosque with four 75m minarets
  • Continental civic plaza accommodating 25,000 worshippers
