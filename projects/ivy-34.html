Gondar Heritage G+10 Hotel

Curvilinear High-Rise Hotel at Urban Intersection

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Gondar, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

Located at a prominent three-way boulevard intersection in the historic imperial city of Gondar. The 10-story hotel features an aerodynamic curvilinear corner facade, rhythmic vertical solar shading louvers, ground floor retail, and a top-floor sky lounge commanding views across the city.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Curvilinear corner landmark facade
  • Passive solar vertical brise-soleil louvers
  • Ground-level street-activated retail arcade
  • Rooftop panoramic sky restaurant
