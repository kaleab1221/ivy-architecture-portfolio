ADRA NGO Regional Headquarters

Corporate Office Pavilion & Executive Suites

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Juba, South Sudan

Architectural Brief & Concept

Regional headquarters for the global humanitarian agency ADRA in Juba. The building pairs an elegant glass facade with horizontal solar brise-soleil shading, accompanied by rich executive interior suites featuring black marble paneling, leather boardroom furnishings, and energy-efficient lighting.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Equatorial solar shading louvers
  • Double-glazed acoustic curtain wall
  • Executive boardroom with bookmatched marble
  • Integrated perimeter security buffer
