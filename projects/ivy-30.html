Anne Academy & Daycare Facility

Early Childhood Learning Center & Play Courtyard

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bishoftu, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A completed early childhood and primary learning academy in Bishoftu. The architecture utilizes colorful educational alphabet murals, secure exterior circulation towers, sheltered playground courtyards, and child-scaled learning environments.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Playful typographic facade branding
  • Enclosed safe playground courtyard
  • External security stair towers
  • Naturally illuminated high-ceiling classrooms
