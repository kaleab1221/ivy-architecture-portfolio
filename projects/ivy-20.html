Gambella University College of Health Compound

Medical Campus & Olympic Stadium Masterplan

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Gambella, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A premier regional higher-education medical hub in Gambella. Featuring state-of-the-art medical classrooms, teaching clinics, a regulation Olympic track and field athletic stadium with spectator grandstands, student housing villages, and tropical shaded canopy walkways.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Olympic standard athletics stadium & grandstand
  • Specialized health science clinical wings
  • Micro-climate passive cooling architecture
  • Dense tree-lined pedestrian circulation spines
