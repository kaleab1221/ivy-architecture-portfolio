Assela Referral Hospital Biophilic Cafe

MCH Teaching Hospital Communal Pavilion

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Assela, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

An inviting social respite for doctors, patients, and families at the Assela Referral Hospital. The biophilic two-story glass pavilion incorporates hanging garden trellis planters, open-air rooftop coffee lounge, and pedestrian crosswalk access.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Biophilic vertical green walls and hanging planters
  • Rooftop outdoor coffee viewing terrace
  • High-transparency glazed social space
  • Direct accessibility to hospital main concourse
