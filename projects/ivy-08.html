Luxury Residence for Hagbes Family

Cantilevered Modernist Estate with Lap Pool

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Bisrate Gebriel, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A modernist private residence in the prestigious Bisrate Gebriel neighborhood. The architecture is defined by dramatic interlocking white cantilevered volumes, floor-to-ceiling glass pavilions, warm timber soffits, an illuminated swimming pool, and terraced landscaped gardens.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Cantilevered upper volume framing pool view
  • Integrated outdoor lounge & sunken pool terrace
  • Warm natural teak wood ceiling soffits
  • Smart home climate and security integration
