Gafat Armament Engineering Defense Complex

Ministry of Defense Masterplan & Helipad Facility

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Dakaka, Bishoftu, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

A high-security defense engineering installation in Bishoftu. The masterplan integrates secure engineering research pavilions, an executive rooftop helipad, an outdoor sports arena with terraced seating, and dense forest security buffers.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Certified rooftop helicopter landing pad
  • Secure engineering laboratories & command center
  • Outdoor amphitheater sports arena
  • Natural forest eco-buffer and access control
