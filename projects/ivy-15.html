Juba Central Fire Brigade Station

Emergency Response & Civic Dispatch Command

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation Juba, South Sudan

Architectural Brief & Concept

The primary emergency fire and rescue hub for Juba, engineered for sub-minute dispatch times. Features a high-bay red structural steel spaceframe canopy housing heavy fire apparatus, observation drill towers, crew living dormitories, and rapid egress slipways.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Long-span red structural truss bay
  • Rapid-dispatch turnaround loop roadway
  • Integrated firefighter quarters & training tower
  • Heavy equipment maintenance shop
