Ministry of Defense Apartment Complex

Curvilinear Multi-Tower Officers Complex

Figure 01 — Primary Elevation 6 Kilo, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

Architectural Brief & Concept

Commissioned for the 6 Kilo district, this twin-tower complex provides secure, dignified housing for defense personnel. Distinctive aerodynamic curved roof forms capture breezes and channel rainwater, connected by high-level communal pedestrian sky bridges.

Key Engineering & Spatial Features

  • Sculptural curved canopy crowns
  • Central sky bridges connecting towers
  • Integrated perimeter security design
  • Abundant open green space & parking
